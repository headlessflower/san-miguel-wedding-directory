San Miguel de Allende is one of the most sought-after destination wedding cities in Mexico.

In this guide, we’ll break down venue types you’ll see most often:

- Haciendas (classic, spacious)
- Boutique hotels (high service, intimate)
- Rooftops (golden hour + city views)
- Gardens (flexible layouts)

## How to choose a venue
Start with these three decisions:

1. Guest count
2. Ceremony style (religious / civil / symbolic)
3. Weather season + backup plan
